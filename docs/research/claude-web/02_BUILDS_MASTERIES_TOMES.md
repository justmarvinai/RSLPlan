# 02 — Builds, masteries and skill tomes

Retrieved 2026-09-17. Gear, stat priorities and mastery presets come from each champion's HellHades page; Ayumilove's set lists are included as a second opinion. Tome counts are counted from Ayumilove's skill-level lists (see 01). Everything under "plan" is analysis by Claude, not a quote from either site.

## Rules that decide most builds

**Warmaster vs Giant Slayer (tier-6 offence mastery).** HellHades' Clan Boss guide: if the champion's A1 hits once or twice, take Warmaster; if it hits three or four times, take Giant Slayer. Applied to this roster, Giant Slayer goes to Apothecary (3-hit A1), Alure (3-hit A1) and Gnishak (3-hit A1). Everyone else takes Warmaster, including Kael — his 4-hit skill is the A3, not the A1.

**Early gear reality.** HellHades' progression guide says an early account lives on Speed, Perception and Lifesteal in 3–5★ gear. Most sets listed on the champion pages (Savage, Cruel, Merciless, Relentless, Protection, Stoneskin) come from dungeon stage 16+ or Hydra and are targets for month 2–4, not week 1. Boots always want SPD; gloves and chest want HP%/DEF% on supports and C.RATE/C.DMG on damage dealers.

**Stat floors that gate content.** Debuffs need accuracy or they simply do not land: roughly 150–180 ACC for dungeon stages 10–16, 220+ for stage 20+. HellHades' Hydra article gives the concrete Normal Hydra numbers: beat 210 SPD to move before the fastest head, about 215 ACC to land debuffs reliably, and 355 RES on the one champion you want to protect from the Head of Mischief's buff steal. Use the HellHades Stages Tool for exact per-stage numbers rather than guessing.

**Masteries to avoid on a future speed-tuned Clan Boss team.** HellHades' speed-tune article names Rapid Response, Arcane Celerity and Cycle of Magic as tune-breakers, and warns that Lasting Gifts can push a buff-heavy team over the 10-buff cap. This matters later — an untuned early "speed team" (Apothecary / High Khatun boosting) does not care.

## Skill tome plan

Owned: 26 Rare, 110 Epic, 12 Legendary, 0 Mythical.

Needed to max everything owned: 24 Rare (Kael 14 + Apothecary 10), ~100 Epic, 85 Legendary, 10 Mythical (Starsage). Legendary tomes are therefore the only real bottleneck, and HellHades' new-player guide is explicit that Legendary tomes cannot be used on a Mythical — Starsage needs Mythical tomes, which come from Hero's Path events and similar.

**Legendary (12 owned) — recommended order**

1. Ultimate Deathknight A3, 2 tomes. Cooldown 5 → 3 on the team Shield + 15% Continuous Heal. Cheapest large upgrade you own.
2. Ultimate Deathknight A2, 4 tomes. Decrease ATK 80% → 100% chance (tomes 2 and 3) and cooldown 4 → 3 (tome 4). Decrease ATK uptime is the single biggest survivability lever in Clan Boss, Ice Golem and Spider.
3. Artak A3, 4 tomes. HP Burn 75% → 100% and cooldown 4 → 3. This is what makes the Spider and Hydra burn plan reliable.
4. Hold the last 2 until the Clan Boss team is settled. Candidates: Artak A2 (Decrease ATK to 100%), Ninja A2 (burn chance per hit), or a better legendary you pull.

**Epic (110 owned)** — you can afford to fully book every Epic you own, so book each one as it joins a team rather than hoarding. Best value per tome first: High Khatun A2 (1 tome, cooldown 4 → 3 on the team speed buff), Godseeker Aniri A3 and passive (3 each, both cooldowns 7 → 4), Tagoar A2 (4 tomes, cooldown 5 → 3), then Uugo A2 and A3 (12 total, Decrease DEF to 100% and lower cooldowns). Alure is last — HellHades rates her book value 4/10 because her turn-meter control works unbooked. Keep 20–30 in reserve for a future Epic pull such as Seer, Royal Guard or Coldheart.

**Rare (26 owned)** — Apothecary first (A2 heal and cooldown, A3 cooldown 5 → 3), then Kael. Rare tomes fit nothing else you own, so there is no reason to save them.

**Mythical (0 owned)** — Starsage's priority per HellHades is A2 (base form Stellar Embryo: heal +20%, then cooldown 4 → 3). Save the first Mythical tomes you earn for that.
