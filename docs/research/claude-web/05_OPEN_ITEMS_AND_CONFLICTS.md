## Things I did not finish researching

1. **Demon Lord speed tunes.** DeadwoodJedi's calculator was not consulted. The Clan Boss team in 03 is an untuned "speed team" only. Whether this roster can reach a 2:1 or 4:3 tune, and at what speeds, is unanswered.
2. **Hydra head-by-head guides.** The six individual head guides (Blight, Suffering, Wrath, Decay, Torment, Mischief) were not read in full; 03 covers the mechanics that interact with your roster, not a complete head playbook. Head-of-Torment counterplay in particular (Perfect Veil, Inquisitor Shamel) needs checking against what Starsage's A3 actually does there.
3. **Chimera Trials.** Deliberately out of scope for an early account, but the trial list and the empowerment breakpoints (7–9 trials, 16–18 trials) are documented on both sites if you later want them.
4. **Clan Boss damage thresholds per difficulty and chest tier.** Hydra's thresholds are captured; the Demon Lord's are not.
5. **Arena validation.** The team in 03 is assembled from ratings and skill text. It has not been checked against current arena defence trends, and Live Arena / Tag Team Arena were not researched at all.
6. **Doom Tower, Faction Wars, Cursed City, Grim Forest, Minotaur, Iron Twins, Sand Devil, Phantom Shogun.** Ratings for these are in 01, but no mechanics or team work was done — they were outside the nine areas requested.
7. **Blessings and Relics.** Both are live systems for this account eventually; HellHades publishes blessing recommendations per champion and a relic tier list, and Ayumilove has a relic and gemstone guide. Not extracted.
8. **Faction Guardians.** Duplicate champions (the day-30 High Khatun, for example) can be assigned for account-wide stats. Not researched.

## Questions that would sharpen the plan

1. Is the account genuinely brand new — created within the last 24 hours and below level 15 — so a new-player promo code is still redeemable?
2. Are you willing to spend at all? Two answers change: whether the Gem Mine and masteries advice is the whole gem plan, and whether a Raid Card's extra XP and Sparring Pit speed is part of the leveling math.
3. How much energy do you actually spend per day (how often you log in) — the 30-day plan assumes roughly two or three sessions a day, not a hard-core schedule.
4. Do you have a clan already, and at what progression level? Chimera and Hydra difficulty, and therefore rewards, depend on the clan, not on you.
5. Do you want the Fire Knight kit (Alure, High Khatun, Apothecary) built inside the first 30 days, or is it fine to push Dragon, Spider and Ice Golem first and start Fire Knight in month two? The current plan takes the second path.
6. Anything from the German client that looks different from these English names, so the website's champion lookup can map both.

## Notes for whoever maintains the data

- Starsage Galathir has two forms; every rating, build and skill list must be stored per form, and his skills are split into base-form, alternate-form and shared (Metamorph).
- Kael, Dark Kael and Supreme Kael are three different champions. Supreme Kael is a Void Legendary released for RAID's 4th anniversary; the account owns plain Kael (Rare, Magic, Dark Elves). Any scraper searching for "Kael" will return all three.
- "High Kathun" in the original request is High Khatun.
- Tome rarity does not cross upward: Legendary tomes cannot be used on Mythical champions. Downgrading (using a higher-rarity tome on a lower-rarity champion) is possible but wasteful.
