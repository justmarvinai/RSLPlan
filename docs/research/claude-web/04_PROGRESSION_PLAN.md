# 04 — Progression plan for a fresh account

Framework from HellHades' new-player guide and Ayumilove's beginner and leveling guides; sequencing and champion choices are analysis by Claude based on 01–03. Dates assume day 1 = 2026-09-17.

## Do these before anything else

**New-player promo code.** Only one can be redeemed per account, and HellHades' promo page states it must be used before level 15 and within 24 hours of account creation (their new-player guide says 72 hours — assume the tighter window). Active new-player codes as of 2026-09-13, one champion each plus brews, potions and 300k silver:

TREEHUGGER (Greenwarden Ruarc) · DESERTQUEEN (Tuhanarak) · GOFAST (Razelvarg) · KITTYCUT (Cheshire Cat) · FIRESTORM (Sicia Flametongue) · MADBOMBER (Gaius the Gleeful) · UNBROKEN (Giath the Truthshield) · GETGRANDOAK (Grand Oak Padraig) · SUPERVICTORY (Pelops the Victor) · UDKING (Ultimate Deathknight) · FORTRESS (Walking Tomb Dreng) · HEARTBREAKER (Cupidus) · GETALICE (Alice the Wanderer) · BIGDAWG (Turvold) · STONECOLD (Thylessia) · STAGSLAYER (Stag Knight) · FINALSPITE (Uugo) · NEMESIS (Akemtum) · HEARTOFCOLD (Coldheart)

Recommendation for this roster: **HEARTOFCOLD** (Coldheart is the strongest general-purpose Rare for Fire Knight, Spider and boss damage) or **FIRESTORM** (Sicia Flametongue is an HP Burn activator and slots straight into the Artak/Ninja/Rathalos burn plan). Skip UDKING and FINALSPITE — you already own those champions. Time-limited codes active on 2026-09-13: PP500 (Plarium Play only), EPYRE, DOROTHY. One code per day.

**Join a clan** at your progression level. Clan Boss keys, Chimera (level 33) and Hydra (level 45) are all clan content, and joining a clan far above you means worse chest rewards for you, not better.

**Gems.** Unlock the Gem Mine (500 gems; it repays itself in roughly 100 days) and spend your first 800 gems on masteries for your first rank-6 champion. Do not buy shards with gems.

Rank-up food cost, for planning: a Legendary needs 5 rank-5 feeders to reach 6★; an Epic needs 4 rank-4 feeders and then 5 rank-5; a Rare needs 3 rank-3, 4 rank-4 and 5 rank-5. Kael and Apothecary are the most expensive champions on your account to six-star, which is the opposite of the usual new-account advice and is entirely because of this roster's rarity mix.

## Days 1–3 — setup

Redeem the promo code, join a clan, start the Arbiter progress missions (they double as a checklist of what the game expects next and hand out shards, silver and gems along the way). Push Normal campaign using your rank-5 Legendaries as they level. Fill every Sparring Pit slot and keep it full from now on — a fully upgraded pit tops a champion up in roughly the time it takes to feed a 1★ champion in the Tavern. Fight your daily Classic Arena battles even if you lose some; medals upgrade the Great Hall, which buffs every champion you will ever own. Book Ultimate Deathknight's A3 (2 legendary tomes) on day one.

## Days 4–14 — farmer online

Move Normal → Hard → Brutal as your clear speed allows. Gear Artak in whatever Speed, Perception and Lifesteal you find, prioritising HP% and SPD, and get him to 6★ and level 60. Level feeders in batches — the Multi-Battle training queue holds 60 champions since the July 2026 update, and Auto-Equip (May 2026) is a fast way to gear fodder. Get UDK to 50, apply his A2 books, and start daily Clan Boss on whatever difficulty you can survive.

## Days 15–21 — dungeons open up

Clan Boss every day, climbing Normal → Hard → Brutal. Take Dragon, Ice Golem and Spider to stage 10 with the core five. Run Minotaur for mastery scrolls (permanent Super Raids there) and the Potion Keeps for ascension materials — Super Raids became permanent in the Keeps in April 2026, which roughly halves the farming time. Ninja to 60. Aim to finish Arbiter missions part 1.

## Days 22–30 — settle the loop

Farm Brutal 12-3 for XP and silver. Rathalos to 60. Push dungeons toward stage 13–16 (stage 16 is where Mystery Shards stop dropping, a useful milestone marker). Start Chimera weekly chests once you hit level 33 — ignore Trials entirely for now, they make the Ultimate form stronger. Start Fire Knight low stages with High Khatun, Apothecary and Alure as they come online. Hold shards for boosted summon events; regular Summon Boosts were raised to 15x and Progressive Chance events to 20x/25x/30x in the September 2026 patch, and a Void x2 event started on 2026-09-18.

## Daily loop from week 2 onward

Clan Boss keys (all of them, on the highest difficulty you can actually beat), Classic Arena battles, Faction Wars in whatever factions you can field, daily and weekly missions, the free daily shop items, and all remaining energy into campaign farming or the dungeon you are currently pushing. Log in for the 3-hour bonus collection when convenient.

## After day 30

HellHades' content order is Dragon 20 first, then Spider 20, then Fire Knight. Dungeon stage 20 is the main gear milestone; stage 21–25 mechanics change (Enemy Max HP damage capped, turn-meter reduction halved), so do not rush past 20 until your gear can handle it.

Level gates to plan around: Doom Tower at 40, Hydra at 45, Cursed City (Sintranos) at 52. Chimera is already open at 33. Grim Forest, added in December 2025, is endgame content — note it exists but ignore it for months.

Mid-term goals in order: a Clan Boss team that reliably kills Brutal, then Nightmare with a real speed tune (use the DeadwoodJedi calculator once you have the champions for it); Dragon 20 on farm; Spider 20; Fire Knight 20; Arbiter missions completed for the free Legendary; Hydra Normal chests once you are level 45 and have six champions worth fielding; then Hard dungeons, where an "Awakened Weakness" passive means your awakening levels start to matter.

## Login rewards worth waiting for

Day 30 is High Khatun (a duplicate for you — use it for Faction Guardians or as skill-up material rather than fodder). Day 60 Yaga the Insatiable, day 90 Dark Athel, day 120 Dark Elhain, day 150 Lordly Legionary, day 180 Scyl of the Drakes. The day 61–90 block also includes a Sacred Shard and a Legendary Skill Tome, which is relevant given legendary tomes are your bottleneck.
