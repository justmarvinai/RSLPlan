# 01 — Game State: RAID Shadow Legends, September 2026

**Compiled:** 2026-09-17 · Patch line **10.x** (10.00 → 10.70+)

Why this document exists: a plan built on 2023 knowledge would be actively harmful. RAID has
changed structurally since then — a new rarity tier above Legendary, a new clan boss, a
whole new equipment axis (Relics), and new dungeon difficulty modes. This is what is true
*now* and what it means for a fresh account.

---

## 1. Rarity tiers

The ladder is now: **Common → Uncommon → Rare → Epic → Legendary → Mythical** `[CONFIRMED]`

**Mythical** is the top tier, introduced above Legendary `[CONFIRMED]`. The game has over a
thousand champions `[LIKELY]`.

### Mythical champions and the Metamorph mechanic

Mythical champions have **two distinct Forms**, each with its own skill set, and sometimes a
different champion *type* between forms `[CONFIRMED]`.

The mechanic is called **Metamorph**. When it triggers `[CONFIRMED]`:
1. The champion **switches Form**
2. **All skill cooldowns reset** (except the Metamorph skill itself)
3. The champion **gets an extra turn**

That third point is the important one. A cooldown reset plus a free turn is an enormous
tempo swing, and it is why Mythicals sit above Legendaries rather than merely alongside them.

**You own one: Starsage Galathir.** See `docs/research/02-champion-dossiers.md`.

## 2. Primal Shards

A shard type that can summon **Rare, Epic, Legendary and Mythical champions of any affinity
except Void** `[CONFIRMED]`.

**Published rates** `[CONFIRMED]`:

| Rarity | Chance |
|---|---|
| Rare | 82.5% |
| Epic | 16% |
| Legendary | 1% |
| **Mythical** | **0.5%** |

**Mercy system** `[LIKELY]`: after **75 summons without a Legendary**, Legendary chance
increases by ~+1% per shard. A separate Mythical mercy threshold exists at around **200
summons** `[UNCERTAIN — exact threshold and increment not confirmed]`.

**2× Primal Summon Boost events** run periodically and double these rates `[CONFIRMED]`.

### What this means for you
**Hold Primal Shards for 2× events.** At 0.5% base, opening them off-event is close to
throwing them away. This is one of the few genuinely free optimisations available to a new
account — it costs nothing but patience. Note also that summon boost rates were increased in
2026: regular Summon Boosts went from 10× to 15×, and Progressive Chance Event boosts from
15×/20×/25× to **20×/25×/30×** `[CONFIRMED]`.

## 3. Relics (Patch 10.00)

A **separate equipment slot** beyond artifacts and accessories `[CONFIRMED]`.

- **One Relic per champion** `[CONFIRMED]`
- Grants **stat bonuses plus a unique Ability** to the wearer `[CONFIRMED]`
- Rarities: **Rare, Epic, Legendary, Mythical** — higher rarity = higher starting Rank and
  more Gemstone Sockets (Rare = Rank 1 … Mythical = Rank 4) `[CONFIRMED]`
- **Crafted in the Forge** using **Ocular Masses**, obtained from fighting the **Chimera**
  `[CONFIRMED]`
- Crafting with only Rare materials can still produce Epic/Legendary/Mythical Relics
  `[CONFIRMED]`
- **Levelled** with **Starstones**; **Ranked up** by sacrificing other Relics or using
  **Basalt** `[CONFIRMED]`
- Removing a Relic costs Starstones; destroying it is free `[CONFIRMED]`

### What this means for you
**Relics are gated behind Chimera, which is gated behind a competent clan and account level
33.** They are a real power axis but they are **not accessible in your first 30 days**. Do
not let Relic content in guides distract you. Noted here so you recognise the system when it
appears, and so you understand one more reason why joining a good clan matters.

---

## 4. Hard Mode Dungeons

Harder versions of the classic dungeons, featuring **"Tainted" bosses** `[CONFIRMED]`.

- Drop **Mythical Artifacts and Accessories** — a rarity previously exclusive to Hydra chests
  `[CONFIRMED]`
- Available from a **wider range of sets and factions**, farmable on demand `[CONFIRMED]`
- Higher stage cleared = better Mythical drop chance and quantity `[CONFIRMED]`

**Relevance to you:** none for months. This is the endgame gear treadmill. It matters only as
context for *why* endgame builds you read about have stats your gear cannot reach.

---

## 5. Super Raids

An **opt-in toggle** on the team selection screen, available for certain dungeons during
limited-time events `[CONFIRMED]`.

- Spends **2× energy** for **2× XP and 2× rewards**, in the time of one run `[CONFIRMED]`
- Works with **Multi-Battle** `[CONFIRMED]`
- **Risk:** a failed run can cost you the doubled runs `[CONFIRMED]`

**Relevance to you: high, and immediate.** This roughly halves the wall-clock time to level
champions when active. It also makes **farmer reliability** a real economic decision rather
than a perfectionist one — a farmer that fails 5% of runs is actively expensive under Super
Raids. Build the farmer to be boring and bulletproof.

---

## 6. Live Arena and Shadow Arena

- **Live Arena** — real-time PvP with expanded daily timeslots (an hour added to each,
  four extra hours per day) `[CONFIRMED]`, and **Live Arena Seasons** added in 10.30
  `[CONFIRMED]`
- **Shadow Arena** — introduced in patch 10.60 `[CONFIRMED]`. `[NOT CONFIRMED]` — detailed
  mechanics were not established and are not load-bearing for your plan
- **Arena Chests** now contain Epic/Legendary/**Mythical** accessories, Mythical Artifacts,
  12 new avatars and 2 avatar frames `[CONFIRMED]`

**Relevance to you:** you asked about **Classic** Arena specifically, which remains the
Great-Hall-feeding mode and the correct early focus. Live Arena is a separate skill-based
mode — worth dabbling in for rewards later, not a day-30 priority.

---

## 7. Chimera (Patch 10.00)

Introduced alongside Relics `[CONFIRMED]`. Full mechanics in
`docs/research/03-content-mechanics.md`. Structurally it is the **second** clan boss
alongside Demon Lord, and it is the gateway to the entire Relic economy.

---

## 8. Champion re-balancing

Patches **10.00** and **10.60** both included champion re-balancing passes `[CONFIRMED]`.

**This is the single biggest risk to the accuracy of any champion evaluation**, including the
ones in this repository. A champion buffed or nerfed in 10.60 may be described using pre-10.60
data on sites that have not been updated.

**Mitigation, and this is a real instruction, not a disclaimer:** before committing a
**Legendary Tome** or a **full mastery set** to a champion, open the champion in-game and
read the actual current skill text. The in-game client is always correct; every website,
including this one, is a lagging indicator.

## 10. The three things that actually changed for a new player

Stripping away the noise, here is what a 2026 fresh account must internalise that a 2023
guide would not tell you:

1. **The gear ceiling rose.** Mythical artifacts and Relics mean endgame builds now hit stat
   numbers that were impossible before. **Do not benchmark your day-30 gear against a content
   creator's showcase build** — you are looking at a different game economy. This is the
   number one cause of new players concluding their account is bad and quitting.

2. **Clan quality became a hard progression gate, not a social preference.** Chimera requires
   10+ level-33 clanmates and 50 weekly activity stars. Relics come from Chimera. Hydra
   requires a clan. **A bad clan now costs you an entire equipment axis.** Joining an active
   clan is a top-5 priority, and it is free.

3. **Mythical rarity changed the tier-list landscape.** Champions rated "S-tier" in 2022 are
   now mid. The counterweight, which matters more to you: **your Starsage Galathir is a
   genuine top-of-the-game champion**, and a fresh account that owns a Mythical is in an
   unusual and strong position. The plan is built around that fact.
