**RES > SPD > HP/DEF** `[LIKELY]`.

This is genuinely unusual and easy to get wrong. If you gear him like a normal support with
ACC substats, you are wasting half his passive.

`[LIKELY]`. Hit SPD and 100% C.RATE first (budget around his own +30% C.RATE), then all-in on

**Books are high value** — A2 and A3 both get −1 cooldown `[LIKELY]`.

**⚠️ Masteries — unresolved.** One source recommends **Warmaster**, but his skills are 2-hit
single-target, which is the classic **Giant Slayer** profile. `[UNCERTAIN]` — **test both
before committing 1,650 scrolls.**

day 5 would be a mistake.

His **Void affinity** is a genuine asset — he never takes an affinity penalty.
