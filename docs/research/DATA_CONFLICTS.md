## A1. 🔴 Does a Skill Tome let you choose which skill to upgrade?

**This is the single most important item in this document**, because my entire earlier tome
plan was built on the answer being "no".

| | Claim |
|---|---|
| **My earlier research** | *"Each tome adds one level to ONE skill, and WHICH skill is upgraded is RANDOM among the skills still needing improvement."* Tagged `[CONFIRMED]` — wrongly, it came from search synthesis |
| **The pack** | Does not state the mechanic explicitly, but its **entire Legendary tome plan presupposes targeting**: *"Ultimate Deathknight A3, 2 tomes"*, *"Ultimate Deathknight A2, 4 tomes"*, *"Artak A3, 4 tomes"*. That plan is incoherent if selection is random |

**My assessment:** the pack is almost certainly right and **I was wrong**. In RAID you select
the specific skill to upgrade. I have rebuilt the tome plan on the targeting assumption.

**Why I am still flagging it:** it took my earlier plan in a completely different direction
(*"never partial-book a champion with dead skills"*), and it is **verifiable by you in about
ten seconds** — open any champion, tap Skills, and see whether the upgrade button is per-skill.

**Consequence if I am wrong and selection IS random:** the pack's plan of spending 2 tomes on
UDK's A3 does not work, and hoarding until you can full-max a champion becomes correct again.

**→ Please confirm: can you pick the skill?**

## B1. 🚨 Rank-up food cost — my build order was built on an inverted premise

**This was my largest single error.**

| | Claim |
|---|---|
| **My earlier plan** | *"Ranking a Rare to 6★ costs far less XP than a Legendary… Building Hurndig first would cost roughly triple for the same day-30 outcome."* Kael was therefore build #1 |
| **The pack** | **Rarity determines starting rank, and starting rank determines food cost.** Mythical starts at **rank 6** (zero feeders). Legendary starts at 5 → needs **5 rank-5 feeders**. Epic starts at 4 → **4 rank-4 then 5 rank-5**. Rare starts at 3 → **3 rank-3, 4 rank-4, then 5 rank-5** |

**So Kael and Apothecary are the most expensive champions on your account to six-star, and
Starsage Galathir is the cheapest — he costs no feeders at all.**

The pack states this explicitly: *"Kael and Apothecary are the most expensive champions on your
account to six-star, which is the opposite of the usual new-account advice and is entirely
because of this roster's rarity mix."*

**Consequence:** the build order is rebuilt from the pack. **Galathir moves from #13 to #1**
and Kael moves from #1 to optional. My reasoning for deferring Galathir — *"the most expensive
champion in the game to level and rank"* — was false on the rank half.

## B2. Campaign farmer: Kael → **Artak**

The pack picks Artak: HellHades ships a dedicated **"Solo Farm Build"** mastery preset for him,
his damage scales off **HP** (cheap, plentiful gear for a new account), and **all three of his
skills are AoE**. He also doubles as the Spider, Hydra, Dragon and Ice Golem carry, so one gear
investment serves five modes.

**Recorded caveat (the pack's own):** Ayumilove rates Artak **4/5** for Campaign, below Gnishak,
Hurndig, Rathalos, Skullcrown, Uugo, Aniri and Kael at **5/5**. Artak is recommended **on
gear-cost grounds, not rating grounds**. Hurndig is the planned later switch for Nightmare 12-3.

first — HellHades explicitly recommends this for early accounts — and do not chase 2:1 or 4:3
until you have the champions and the DeadwoodJedi calculator. Adopted.

## B8. 🚨 Starsage Galathir cannot use Legendary Skill Tomes

New from the pack, and important: **Legendary tomes cannot be used on a Mythical champion.**
Galathir needs **Mythical Tomes** — 10 of them — which come from Hero's Path events and
similar. **You own 0.**

Tome rarity does not cross upward. Downgrading (a higher-rarity tome on a lower-rarity
champion) is possible but wasteful.

## B9. Galathir's passives were mis-assigned

Mine: a single passive I called *"Stellar Reflection"*, described as the base-form passive.
Pack: **two separate passives.**
- **Base form — "Eternal Beyonder"**: −5% damage taken per 100 RES (caps 25%); prevents death
  at 1 HP on a fatal hit, then equalises his HP to the team average
- **Alternate form — "Starlight Reflect"**: **+ACC equal to 75% of his RES** when placing
  debuffs; can reflect CC debuffs back at a higher-ACC enemy

My "build RES not ACC" conclusion survives — but it is the **alternate form's** passive that
does the RES→ACC conversion, not the base form's.
