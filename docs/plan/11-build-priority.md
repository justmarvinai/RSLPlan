# 11 — Champion Build Priority

**Date:** 2026-09-17

You asked not to have to build twenty champions at once. **You do not.** You need to build
**six**, in a specific order, and everything else waits.

---

## The rule this document enforces

> **One well-built champion does more for your account than five half-built ones.**
> `[CONFIRMED as consensus across every 2026 beginner guide]`

Resources in RAID do not spread — they compound. A champion at level 60, 6★, with full
masteries and decent gear is worth more than three champions at level 40 with nothing. The
three at 40 clear nothing; the one at 60 unlocks the content that funds the next one.

---

| # | Champion | Role | Start | Why now |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Kael** | Campaign farmer | **Day 1** | Generates resources instead of consuming them |
| **2** | **Apothecary** | Healer / speed | **Day 3** | 10 Rare Tomes for a heal + AoE speed buff. Cheapest power on the roster |
| **3** | **Ultimate Deathknight** | Survivability anchor | **Day 7** | 30% DEF aura + Dec ATK + Shield + damage redirect keeps a bad team alive |
| **4** | **Uugo** | Debuff engine | **Day 12** | 60% AoE Decrease DEF — the largest damage multiplier available |
| **5** | **Artak** | Damage + HP Burn | **Day 16** | HP-scaling (forgiving gear), AoE Dec ATK, and **your only AoE HP Burn** |

**Everyone else waits until Day 30+.** That is not neglect; it is the plan working.

## Why this order, champion by champion

### 1. Kael — build first, no alternatives considered
**Target: level 60 · 6★ · Lifesteal set · solo Brutal 12-3 on auto at 100%**

He is the only champion on the roster who **generates** resources rather than consuming them.
Every other build is funded by him.

**Why Kael and not Hurndig** (who can also solo-farm, on Nightmare):
Ranking a **Rare** to 6★ costs far less XP than a Legendary, and he does the job. Hurndig is
the *upgrade*, not the *starter*. Building Hurndig first would cost roughly triple for the
same day-30 outcome.

- **Gear:** Lifesteal (self-sustain, no healer needed) + Speed
- **Stats: HP, DEF, SPD, ACC** — *survivability, not damage.* The metric is unattended
  reliability, not maximum damage. A 95% farmer breaks Multi-Battle and loses doubled runs
  under Super Raids
- **Masteries:** Ruthless Ambush · Cycle of Violence · Kill Streak · Spirit Haste ·
  **Cycle of Revenge** `[LIKELY]`
- **🔑 Spend your first 800 Gems buying his masteries outright.** Multiple 2026 guides
  independently call this *"one of the highest-value gem expenditures in the game"*
  `[CONFIRMED]`. It skips the entire Minotaur bootstrap problem
- **⚠️ 6★ him BEFORE masteries** — Tier 6 masteries are locked behind Rank 6 `[CONFIRMED]`

---

### 2. Apothecary — the cheapest power gain you will ever get
**Target: level 60 · 6★ · triple Speed sets · 10 Rare Tomes · 100% C.RATE**

- **10 Rare Tomes fully max him** `[LIKELY]` — **you hold 26.** Nothing else on this roster
  converts so little into so much
- **His heal scales off the ALLY's MAX HP, not his own** — so he heals properly while badly
  geared. Almost nothing else you own is that forgiving early
- **AoE Increase SPD + AoE Turn Meter fill** is exactly the tempo package a new account needs
- **Masteries: GIANT SLAYER, not Warmaster** `[CONFIRMED]` — his A1 hits 3 times and Giant
  Slayer procs per hit. Plus **Lore of Steel** (more speed from triple Speed sets) and
  **Lasting Gifts** (extends the Increase SPD buff)
- **Push C.RATE to 100%** — Critical Heals take his heal from 35% → 52.5%, and it feeds Giant
  Slayer procs

---

### 3. Ultimate Deathknight — the reason your team stops dying
**Target: level 60 · 6★ · HP/DEF gear · high RES**

Your team is going to be badly geared for weeks. UDK is the champion that makes that survivable:

- **+30% ally DEF aura in all battles** — free stats on everyone
- **AoE 50% Decrease ATK at 80% chance** — the best defensive debuff in the game, and it is on
  an *Epic-tier reliability* number
- **Team Shield + 15% Continuous Heal**
- **Redirects a full hit off any ally onto himself** (100%, 50% vs bosses, not vs AoE)
- **He gets stronger as allies die** (+10% HP/DEF/SPD each) — so he *wins* the fights he is
  losing

**And he is your future solo farmer.** Later, re-gear him into **Toxic** and he solos Dragon 25
and Ice Golem 25 `[CONFIRMED]`. **Force affinity means he is advantaged at Ice Golem 25.**

- **Masteries: Defence primary, Support secondary. No Warmaster/Giant Slayer** — he is not a
  damage mastery champion
- **Long-term stat targets for solo Ice Golem:** ~225 ACC, ~225 RES, ~210 SPD, max HP/DEF

---

### 4. Uugo — your damage multiplier
**Target: level 60 · 6★ · Perception/Speed · 200+ SPD · as much ACC as you can find · booked A2**

**60% AoE Decrease DEF is the single largest damage increase available to you**, and her A2
delivers **Block Buffs on the same skill**. She is also a healer, a cleanser and an emergency
revive.

- **🔑 Book her A2 first.** Fully booked it goes to **100% debuff chance on a 3-turn cooldown**
  `[CONFIRMED]` — sources describe this as the difference between "fine" and "team-defining."
  **This is your #1 Epic Tome target, and you hold 110 of them**
- **Stats: SPD > ACC > HP% > DEF%.** HP is an *offensive* stat for her — her heal scales off
  her own MAX HP
- **Ascension matters:** Stage 3 gives +10 ACC, Stage 6 gives +10% HP `[LIKELY]`
- **Honest caveat:** she needs real ACC to function, and you will not have it for a few weeks.
  Build her anyway — she improves as your gear does, and she never leaves your roster

---

### 5. Artak — forgiving damage, and the key to two dungeons
**Target: level 60 · 6★ · HP/SPD/ACC · Regeneration or Perception**

- **He scales off MAX HP, not ATK** — and HP gear is the easiest gear to find early. He
  performs while under-geared, which almost no damage dealer does
- **He gets stronger the longer a fight runs**, which is the shape of every under-geared fight
- **AoE 50% Decrease ATK** — a second source, stacking your survivability
- **🔑 He is your ONLY AoE HP Burn.** That makes him:
  - **the win condition at Spider 21–25** (burning spiderlings deal 3% of the boss's MAX HP
    each on their turn — ~30% per rotation with 10 of them)
  - **the enabler for Rathalos's passive** (+50% damage, −25% damage taken vs burning targets)
  - **DoT pressure at Ice Golem**, which skips the revive mechanic entirely
- Eventually **solos Ice Golem Hard 5** `[CONFIRMED]`

### 6. Tagoar *or* High Khatun — pick one, not both
**Target: level 60 · 6★**

Both provide AoE Increase SPD. **Build one now and the other after day 30.**

| | **Tagoar** | **High Khatun** |
|---|---|---|
| AoE Increase SPD | **3-turn cooldown** ✅ | 4-turn cooldown |
| Aura | +25% ally HP, all battles | **+19% ally SPD, all battles** ✅ |
| Also brings | Mass revive · AoE Shield · AoE heal · Increase DEF on A1 | AoE TM fill · Dec SPD · AoE Dec TM |
| Endgame | **Long-lived** — Hydra, Orc Faction Wars ✅ | Limited — "not an endgame staple" |
| Books | Unknown | 11, A2 priority |

**Recommendation: Tagoar**, for three reasons — the 3-turn cooldown is what actually sustains a
speed tune, he brings a full sustain package alongside it, and he stays relevant at endgame
while High Khatun does not.

**Build High Khatun instead if** you intend to push Arena early — her 19% all-battles speed
aura is the better *leader* aura, and she is the engine of the well-known early 2:1 Clan Boss
tune `[CONFIRMED]`.

**Best answer: build Tagoar for the kit, and keep High Khatun at a low level purely as an aura
leader.** An unlevelled champion still provides their aura. That is nearly free.

## ⚠️ Why Starsage Galathir is not #1 — read this before overruling me

He is the best champion you own and probably the best champion in the game. Deferring him looks
wrong. Here is the reasoning:

1. **He is a Mythical — the most expensive champion in the game to level and rank.** On day 5,
   that same XP and silver produces a finished Kael *and* a finished Apothecary
2. **His kit is insurance, not acceleration.** Mass revive, cleanse and Block Debuffs prevent
   losses. They do not make Brutal 12-3 clear faster, and speed is what you need in week one
3. **His data is the thinnest on the roster.** Base stats, aura, sets, masteries and book
   requirements are all `[NOT CONFIRMED]`. Committing scarce resources to unverified builds is
   exactly what `CLAUDE.md` forbids
4. **His build is unusual and easy to get wrong.** You stack **RESISTANCE, not Accuracy** —
   his passive converts RES into ACC. Gearing him like a normal support wastes half his passive.
   Better to build him once, correctly, with real gear

**When to build him:** once Dragon is farmable and you have gear worth putting on him — roughly
**day 45–60**. Then he becomes your permanent anchor for Chimera, Hydra, Doom Tower and Arena.

**If you disagree and want him now, say so and I will re-sequence the plan around it.** It is a
defensible choice — it is just slower to day-30 outcomes.

## Mastery priority — the scarcest thing you own

A full mastery set costs **1,650 scrolls** (100 Basic / 600 Advanced / 950 Divine)
`[LIKELY]`, farmable **only in Minotaur's Labyrinth**, and it is slow. **You cannot mastery
eighteen champions.**

**Order:**
1. **Kael** — buy with **800 Gems**, do not farm
2. **Apothecary** — Giant Slayer
3. **Ultimate Deathknight** — Defence/Support
4. **Uugo** — Warmaster
5. **Artak** — Warmaster
6. **Tagoar** — Support/Defence

**🔑 The mechanic that makes this tractable:** build a **4-champion Minotaur team that is
already fully mastered, then put the champion you are farming for in the 5th slot.** Maxed
champions stop receiving scrolls, so everything funnels into slot five `[CONFIRMED]`. This
roughly quadruples your farm rate. **Start only once you can auto Minotaur stage 13+; farm
stage 15.**

### 🚨 Two mastery traps that cost real resources
1. **Never assign masteries to a champion below Rank 6** — Tier 6 (Warmaster / Giant Slayer) is
   locked behind 6★, and they are the only masteries that really matter `[CONFIRMED]`
2. **Never take Rapid Response, Arcane Celerity or Cycle of Magic on a speed-tuned Clan Boss
   champion** — they grant Turn Meter or cut cooldowns unpredictably and desynchronise the
   rotation `[CONFIRMED]`. **Decide whether a champion is a tuned CB champion BEFORE assigning
   masteries**, because undoing it costs a full re-farm

## What "built" actually means

A champion is **built** when all five are true:

- [ ] **Level 60**
- [ ] **6★** (required before Tier 6 masteries exist)
- [ ] **Ascended** far enough to equip accessories
- [ ] **Full masteries** appropriate to their role
- [ ] **Gear that hits their stat targets** — not just gear in every slot

Partial progress on six champions is worth less than full completion on three. **When in doubt,
finish something.**
