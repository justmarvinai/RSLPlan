# 10 — Roster Analysis

**Date:** 2026-09-17 · Roster: 1 Mythical · 7 Legendary · 8 Epic · 2 Rare

This document answers one question: **what can this roster actually do, and what can it not?**
Everything downstream — build order, teams, the 30-day plan — falls out of this.

---

## 1. The honest headline

**This is not a normal fresh account, and the plan should not treat it like one.**

A genuinely new F2P account has two Legendaries at best. You have a **Mythical plus seven
Legendaries plus eight Epics**, and **110 Epic Tomes** — which is a mid-game stockpile, not a
starter one.

**What that means:** your bottleneck is **not champions**. You could not meaningfully improve
this roster with better pulls right now. Your bottleneck is **gear, levels, silver, mastery
scrolls and account level** — pure resources.

**The strategic consequence, and it is the most important line in this repository:**

> **Do not build champions. Build a resource engine, then spend its output on a small number
> of champions.** Every hour spent levelling a sixth champion before your farmer is finished
> is an hour stolen from the thing that makes all other hours cheaper.

The single most likely way you waste this roster is by trying to build all eighteen champions
at once because they all look good. They *are* all good. That is exactly the trap.

more to you than it would be to an established account, because **sustain is what lets
under-geared champions survive content their stats say they should lose.** You can brute-force
your way into dungeons earlier than your gear justifies.

Decrease DEF is the single biggest damage multiplier in the game; Decrease ATK is the single

This matters specifically because **Clan Boss speed tuning is gated on sustaining AoE Increase
SPD**, and most accounts have one source at best. **A 2:1 Clan Boss tune is realistically

## 3. Where this roster is genuinely weak

### 3.1 🚨 Fire Knight — your hardest problem, but not unsolvable

Fire Knight needs **12 separate hits per rotation** to strip Fyro's shield, and the hits must
come mostly from **A1s** because A1 is always available.

**Hit-count audit — A1 hits on the boss, per champion, per turn:**

| Champion | A1 hits | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Apothecary** | **3** | "Scatterbolt", 3 random hits |
| **Alure** | **3** | "Psychic Whip", 3 random hits |
| **Gnishak** | **3** | "Pestilent Censer", 3 random hits |
| Morag Bronzelock | 2 | "Raw Iron Slab" |
| Tagoar | 2 | "Magic Stick" |
| Turvold | 2 | "Crackling Blade" |
| Skullcrown | 2 | AoE + extra hit above 50% HP |
| Everyone else | 1 | — |

**Best four:** Apothecary + Alure + Gnishak + Morag = **11 hits**. Add Skullcrown or Tagoar and
you clear 12. Counterattack sets (**Retaliation / Avenging**) add free hits on top.

**So Fire Knight is reachable — but the team depends on Alure and Gnishak, and both are
gear-hungry.** Alure needs **100% C.RATE and 300+ ACC**; Gnishak needs real stats.

> **Verdict: Fire Knight is a month 2–4 project, not a 30-day one.** Expect stage 10–13 by day
> 30 and treat high stages as a later goal. **This is a gear problem, not a roster problem** —
> which is good news, because gear is farmable and champions are not.

### 3.2 No team-wide Unkillable, Block Damage or Counterattack
The standard endgame Ultra-Nightmare Clan Boss templates are built on **Unkillable rotations**
or **Counterattack teams**. You have neither.

**This is less bad than it sounds.** Your alternative is **revive-and-sustain**, and you have
six sources of it. It is a slower, less elegant route to the same place, and it will hold you
back at true UNM rather than in the first several months.

**If you pull an Unkillable or Block Damage provider, tell me** — it would change the Clan Boss
plan materially. This is the single most valuable gap to fill from summons.

### 3.3 No team-wide Increase ATK
Only Turvold self-buffs. Ice Golem specifically wants **Increase ATK (or Increase DEF for
DEF-scaling teams) plus Increase SPD**.

**Mitigation you already have:** run Ice Golem as a **DEF-scaling team** — Morag (DEF-scaling
damage), UDK (DEF-scaling damage and shields), Aniri (Defence type) — and use **Tagoar's
Increase DEF** instead. This works, and it happens to be gear-efficient.

### 3.4 ⚠️ Affinity imbalance — Magic-heavy, and it bites at Dragon 25

**Spread: Magic ×9 · Force ×4 · Void ×3 · Spirit ×2**

| Content | Boss affinity | You are… |
|---|---|---|
| **Dragon 25** | **Force** | ⚠️ **Your nine Magic champions are DISADVANTAGED.** Only Morag and High Khatun (Spirit) are advantaged |
| **Ice Golem 25** | **Magic** | ✅ Your four **Force** champions are **advantaged** — and UDK and Gnishak, your two Ice Golem solo candidates, are both Force |
| Fire Knight / Spider | Varies | — |

**Two practical consequences:**
1. **For Dragon 25, field Force and Void champions** — Galathir, Rathalos, Gnishak, UDK
   (Force, neutral), Turvold, Aniri, Skullcrown (Void, neutral), Morag, High Khatun (Spirit,
   advantaged). **Leave the Magic crowd at home.**
2. **Your three Void champions — Turvold, Godseeker Aniri, Skullcrown — are worth more than
   their tier ratings suggest**, because they never take a penalty anywhere. On a roster this
   small, affinity flexibility has real value.

neatly onto Ice Golem's revive mechanic. **Until verified, he is excluded from every team.**

## 6. What to chase in summons

Given the gaps above, these are what would actually move the needle — in priority order:

1. **A team-wide Unkillable or Block Damage provider** — your single biggest structural gap.
   Unlocks standard endgame Clan Boss templates
2. **A team-wide Counterattack provider** — helps Clan Boss *and* Fire Knight shield-breaking
3. **More multi-hit A1 champions** — directly fixes Fire Knight
4. **A team-wide Increase ATK buffer** — Ice Golem and general damage
5. **Spirit and Force champions** — to rebalance a Magic-heavy roster, Dragon 25 especially

**What you do NOT need:** more Decrease DEF, more revives, more healers, more single-target
damage. You are deep in all four. **A new Legendary in those categories changes nothing** — and
I will tell you so plainly rather than manufacturing a reason to re-plan.
